The RIDE LMS Training Path

Three levels of training that build on each other — all delivered through a modern, mobile‑friendly LMS tailored to your operation.

Tier 1 — RIDE Safety Orientation (Base Level)

Required for every employee before any on‑site training.

This module builds the foundation for safe, consistent operations across your entire team.

Employees learn:

  • RIDE Framework (Recognize, Identify, Decide, Execute)
  • Basic safety expectations
  • Code of conduct
  • Pre‑shift readiness
  • Guest courtesy fundamentals
