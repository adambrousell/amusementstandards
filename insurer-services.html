THE UNDERWRITING BLIND SPOT
The operator knows their daily reality. You see an annual application.
WHAT UNDERWRITERS CURRENTLY SEE
Lagging Indicators
  • Annual application with self-reported financial and operational data
  • Loss-run history spanning 3–5 years
  • Periodic loss control site visits — typically annual or biennial
  • ASTM F24 compliance certifications and state inspection records
  • Third-party ride inspection reports
WHAT AS 360 SHOWS YOU
Leading Indicators
  • Real-time staffing levels relative to operational load
  • Daily and weekly inspection completion rates and findings
  • Weather-adjusted operational decisions and capacity management
  • Queue stress patterns that correlate with incident frequency
  • Maintenance cycle adherence vs. manufacturer-recommended intervals
  • Near-miss and minor incident trends before they escalate to claims
  • Staff training currency and certification expiration patterns
  • Service bulletin compliance timelines
THE COST OF THE BLIND SPOT
Three consequences for your book.
🎯
Adverse Selection
Without continuous risk visibility, you cannot distinguish between a well-run facility with strong operational discipline and a poorly run facility with a clean loss history that has simply been lucky. The next claim is always a surprise.
💰
Mispriced Risk
When all facilities of similar size and attraction mix receive comparable pricing, the best operators subsidize the worst. Premium adequacy depends on risk differentiation that current tools cannot provide.
🔄
Reactive Loss Control
Traditional loss control identifies issues after a site visit and recommends corrections that may or may not be implemented. There is no mechanism for continuous monitoring or early-warning detection.
