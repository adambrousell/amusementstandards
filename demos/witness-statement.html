Witness Statement Workflow

From QR scan to investigation file — fully documented chain of custody

Witness Scans QR Code

Location-specific QR code opens mobile-optimized statement form. No app needed — works in any smartphone browser. Location and timestamp auto-populate.

WITNESS ACTION
✍️

Statement Written (3–5 min)

Witness provides their name, role, contact info, selects incident type, and writes a free-text account of what they observed. Guided prompts help capture key details.

WITNESS ACTION
📸

Photo Evidence Attached

Witness uploads up to 3 photos directly from their camera or gallery — scene conditions, equipment state, injuries, signage, or anything relevant to the incident.

WITNESS ACTION (OPTIONAL)
✅

Accuracy Confirmation & Consent

Witness confirms statement accuracy and acknowledges it may be used for investigation, insurance, and regulatory purposes. Creates a verifiable attestation record.

Submission & Reference Number

Statement submitted with unique reference (WS-2026-0089). Instant confirmation displayed. Timestamped and immutable — cannot be edited after submission.
