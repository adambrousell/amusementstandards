NJAC 5:14A-4.13: New Jersey requires different actions based on incident severity. Determine category IMMEDIATELY to provide correct guidance to caller.

⚠️ CATEGORY A: SHUT DOWN & IMMEDIATE REPORT

If ANY of these occurred, facility MUST:

  1. Shut down the ride and secure the area
  2. Evacuate riders and provide care for injured
  3. Report to NJ DCA IMMEDIATELY by phone
  4. Submit Incident Report within 24 hours
  5. Send copy of report to ride manufacturer

⚡ CATEGORY B: REPORT WITHIN 24 HOURS

If ANY of these occurred, facility MUST:

  1. Report to NJ DCA within 24 hours (phone or email)
  2. Submit written incident report within 5 days
  3. Repair per manufacturer instructions
  4. Submit written report of corrective actions

📋 CATEGORY C: RECORD ONLY (No State Report Required)

Facility MUST keep internal record including:

  • Name and address of injured party
  • Age and sex
  • Brief description of injury
  • Ride where injury occurred
  • Time, date, weather conditions
  • Description of incident

6 Immediate Actions Required (Tell Caller)

CRITICAL: Based on incident category, caller must take specific immediate actions per NJ regulations.

⚠️ CATEGORY A IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:

  1. DO NOT restart the ride - It must remain shut down until NJ DCA authorizes
  2. Call NJ DCA IMMEDIATELY: (609) 984-7834
  3. Preserve the scene - Do not move equipment, clean, or repair anything
  4. Document everything - Photos, videos, witness info, weather
  5. Notify ride manufacturer - Required by NJAC 5:14A-4.13
  6. Submit Incident Report Form within 24 hours to NJ DCA

⚡ CATEGORY B IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:

  1. Report to NJ DCA within 24 hours: (609) 984-7834 or email
  2. Prepare written incident report - Must be submitted within 5 days
  3. Document the malfunction/evacuation - Photos, details, what happened
  4. Do not operate ride until repaired per manufacturer instructions
  5. Submit corrective action report to NJ DCA after repairs

📋 CATEGORY C IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:

  1. Create internal record - Must include all NJAC required fields
  2. Keep record accessible - Must be available for DCA inspection
  3. No state report required - But record must be maintained
  4. Evaluate if incident reveals pattern - Multiple minor incidents may require action
