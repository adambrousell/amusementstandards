Ongoing Managed Services

Continuous safety, compliance, and operational support—because amusement operations never stop.

Request This Program

Who This Is For

Ongoing Managed Services is designed for parks, FECs, and attractions that have completed initial safety and compliance work but need continuous support to maintain standards, adapt to regulatory changes, manage staff turnover, and respond to incidents. If you're looking for a trusted partner to handle your safety, training, and compliance workload on an ongoing basis—without hiring full‑time specialists—this program is for you.

Perfect for operators who need:

  • Regular training refreshers as new staff join and existing staff need recertification
  • Quarterly or monthly risk reviews to catch issues before they become incidents
  • SOP updates as operations change or new attractions are added
  • Incident trend monitoring to identify patterns and prevent repeat issues
  • Regulatory update alerts so you stay compliant without constantly tracking changes
  • On‑demand support when urgent questions, audits, or incidents arise

…this subscription‑based program gives you predictable, proactive support year‑round.

What's Included

We become an extension of your team, providing continuous oversight, training, updates, and incident support based on your chosen service tier.

All tiers include:

  • Regulatory update alerts and compliance deadline tracking
  • Annual training content refreshers and new hire onboarding support
  • Incident trend monitoring with quarterly or monthly reporting
  • SOP review and update recommendations as operations evolve
  • Priority email and phone support for urgent questions
  • Access to our training library and digital inspection tools

Outcomes

By partnering with us on an ongoing basis, you'll have:

Consistent compliance without constant vigilance

We track regulatory changes, deadlines, and updates so you don't have to—keeping you compliant without the administrative burden.

Trained staff even with high turnover

New hire training, recertification, and refresher courses are handled continuously, so your team stays sharp regardless of staffing changes.

Proactive risk identification

Regular reviews catch operational drift, procedural gaps, and incident trends before they escalate into serious problems.

Up‑to‑date SOPs and documentation

Your operating procedures, training materials, and compliance documents stay current as your business evolves.

Expert support when you need it

No more scrambling to find answers during audits, incidents, or regulatory inquiries—we're already part of your team.
