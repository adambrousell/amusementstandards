Who Is the CRC

The Manufacturer's Qualified Person of Record at Every Installation

Not a line-level operator. Not a badge everyone earns. The CRC is the single person at each site accountable for the correct operation of every manufacturer ride at their park — the documented qualified person of record that doesn't exist today.

🎯
What the CRC Does
The person who oversees the ride. Accountable for pre-opening inspection, restraint verification, emergency response, and on-site documentation. Oversight role — not required to be physically present at the ride at all times. One CRC per site, covering all manufacturer rides at that location.
🏢
For Parks With Existing Training
Parks with internal training programs can credential their existing lead staff as CRCs — no LMS replacement, no retraining line-level operators. The CRC is a manufacturer endorsement on top of what they already have. It credentials the person, not the program.
📄
Why It Matters Legally
In the absence of a documented manufacturer training program, incident liability shifts toward design and engineering. The CRC puts a manufacturer-defined qualified person of record on file at every installation — closing the documentation gap before the incident happens.
🔑
One Credential, One Price
$500/person/year per site. Covers all manufacturer rides at that location. One course with ride-specific modules for every unit at the park. One annual recertification. The quarterly certification report auto-generates — serves as both compliance record and billing artifact.

Regulatory Foundation

ASTM F770-19 Alignment

The CRC maps directly to the ASTM standard for ownership, operation, maintenance, and inspection of amusement rides. OSHA 1910.147 (LOTO) is incorporated under §8.2.

Section
What the Standard Requires
How the CRC Addresses It
§7.1
Operator must perform and document a structured pre-opening inspection before each operating day.
CRC course includes ride-specific pre-opening inspection checklist module. Controller signs off daily. Record retained in LMS.
§8.1
Operators must be trained in proper ride operation and applicable guest safety rules. Training must be documented.
CRC covers full ride systems, restraint verification, rider screening, and guest communication. Completion certificate with unique ID issued on 80% pass.
§8.2
Operator is responsible for safe loading, restraint verification, emergency response, and LOTO compliance during maintenance.
CRC includes LOTO per OSHA 1910.147, emergency stop and evacuation procedures, and incident command basics. 20-question final exam.
§8.3
Training must include a supervised field demonstration. The evaluator must be qualified and must document task performance.
CRC includes §8.3 documentation protocol and supervised demonstration facilitation. The Controller is the manufacturer's qualified person of record — 1 per site.
Full §8 Compliance Note

The CRC addresses the manufacturer's qualified-person-of-record requirements. For full §8 compliance covering all operators and attendants at the ride, parks add the optional Attendant ($1,500/ride type) and Operator ($2,500/ride type) SCORM modules to create a complete training package on the same platform.
