What ASDE-X Doesn't Do

Radar tracks position.
It doesn't monitor voice.

LaGuardia's ASDE-X knew where every aircraft and vehicle was on the surface. What it couldn't do — what no system at most airports does — is monitor all radio channels simultaneously and flag when ground ops and local control aren't coordinating in real time.

What the NTSB is now investigating

NTSB Chairwoman Jennifer Homendy confirmed investigators are reviewing whether the ARFF truck was visible on the ASDE-X display at the time of the crash, and whether any automated alerts were generated. A retired ATC expert explained the incident reveals a coordination gap between the ground controller managing the ARFF truck and the local controller managing Jazz 8646's landing — two controllers, two channels, one runway, no unified voice picture.
