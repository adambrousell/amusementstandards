The Coverage Gap

ATC is recorded. Everything else disappears.

Federal regulation mandates ATC voice recording. Ground ops, ARFF, medical, ramp, and airline ops channels — where responder coordination happens during emergencies — are unmonitored at most airports.

CH-01 · ATC Tower
Air Traffic Control
Aircraft separation, runway assignments, emergency declarations. Federally mandated recording and archival.
RECORDED — FAA MANDATED
CH-02 · Ground Control
Ground Movement
Taxiway routing, pushback, runway crossings. Critical during emergency diversions and ground stops.
UNMONITORED AT MOST AIRPORTS
CH-04 · ARFF
Aircraft Rescue & Fire
ARFF unit dispatch, positioning, access route calls. Response time data exists only in memory.
UNMONITORED — RESPONSE DATA LOST
CH-06 · Medical
Airport Medical Response
EMS staging, mutual aid activation, patient count coordination. Zero structured record today.
UNMONITORED — NO RECORD
CH-07 · Airport Ops
Operations & Command
Passenger counts, gate assignments, airline notification. The command channel during any incident.
UNMONITORED AT MOST AIRPORTS
CH-09 · Ramp / Cargo
Ramp Operations
Ground equipment, fuel, baggage. Often the first to visually observe an aircraft anomaly on approach.
NEVER MONITORED
