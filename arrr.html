Platform

Composite scoring for amusement attraction risk

AS 360 scores individual attractions, then rolls those scores into a property-level composite weighted by risk tier. The result is a single, defensible number that tracks your operation over time — built for the people who underwrite, regulate, and litigate amusement risk.

R
Readiness
Staff qualifications, training completion, credential currency, and pre-operational verification. Measures whether the people running the attraction are prepared to do so.
I
Integrity
Mechanical condition, maintenance history, manufacturer compliance, and inspection documentation. Measures whether the attraction itself is in proper working order.
D
Delivery
Operational execution during live operation — dispatch procedures, load/unload protocols, queue management, and real-time safety compliance adherence.
E
Environment
Physical surroundings, egress, signage, lighting, ADA compliance, weather protocols, and the condition of the built environment around each attraction.
How It Works

Assess. Score. Monitor.

Every AS 360 engagement follows the same three-step process, regardless of facility size or type.

01
Assess
On-site or desktop assessment against each pillar. Every attraction at the facility is evaluated individually using standardized criteria.
02
Score
Per-attraction scores roll into a property-level composite, weighted by risk tier. You receive a Facility Score Certificate with pillar breakdowns and prioritized findings.
03
Monitor
Re-score quarterly or annually. Closed findings lift pillar scores. The trend line over time is the signal your insurer and your own operation need to see.
Intelligence Products

Complementary data systems

Standalone intelligence products that extend visibility beyond the four walls of a facility.

Signal Intelligence
VoxIntel
Real-time radio signal capture and classification for operational awareness. Detects safety-relevant communication patterns across live operations without requiring staff behavior changes or hardware on the attraction itself.
  • Passive signal capture — no operator-side installation
  • Automated event classification and severity tagging
  • Continuous monitoring feed with historical lookback
Regulatory Intelligence
RegScan
Public regulatory and incident intelligence aggregation across federal and state sources. Continuously monitors the public record for signals relevant to amusement operations — inspections, incidents, litigation, and legislative changes.
  • CPSC, OSHA, state ride safety databases, court records
  • Legislative tracking across all 50 states
  • Facility-matched alerting for portfolio monitoring
