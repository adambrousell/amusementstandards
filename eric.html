About Eric

A Professional Built on Service

Eric Magliacane is a licensed New Jersey real estate salesperson certified through the NJ Department of Banking & Insurance — Real Estate Commission. He brings a background that few agents can match: years in customer-focused hospitality environments have sharpened his instinct for listening, adapting, and delivering results that feel personal.

Whether you're buying your first home on the Jersey Shore or selling a commercial property, Eric approaches every deal with the same commitment: efficient, respectful, and honest representation from first conversation to closed transaction.

Rooted in the Monmouth and Ocean County communities he serves, Eric combines deep local market knowledge with the national reach of eXp Realty's platform.
