Why Pre-Launch Matters

Opening Day Is the Riskiest Day You'll Ever Have at That Ride.

A new installation is an unfamiliar ride for every operator, every supervisor, and every maintenance tech at the park. Training done in a classroom two weeks before opening is not the same as supervised operation at the actual ride. The gap between those two things is where incidents happen.

The Pre-Launch Intensive puts an Amusement Standards expert on-site, at the ride, in the operating window — running live certification, supervising field demonstrations, and producing documentation that proves the park was ready before Day 1.

The Manufacturer's Perspective

As the manufacturer, you sold the ride. If an incident happens in the first season and the park never had documented operator certification on record, the question becomes yours to answer. The Pre-Launch Intensive creates the documentation that shows your standard was met before the ride ever opened.

What This Delivers for the Park

Parks that go through the Pre-Launch Intensive open with a complete pre-opening compliance package — signed CRC certifications, field demo records, emergency drill documentation, and a pre-opening inspection sign-off. That package is the difference between "we trained our people" and "here is the documentation that proves it."

Live Certification at the Ride

What's Included

Four Deliverables. One On-Site Engagement.

The Pre-Launch Intensive is structured around four core deliverables — all produced on-site, all documented, all delivered before the ride opens.

Deliverable 01
🎯
Live CRC Certification — Full Cohort
  • All CRC candidates certified at the ride during the Intensive window
  • Final exam administered on-site — 80% pass threshold
  • Failed candidates receive remediation before re-test
  • Signed CRC certificates issued same day as passing
  • Completion data recorded in AS portal — quarterly report updated immediately
  • No candidate leaves uncertified without a documented remediation plan
Deliverable 02
🔍
ASTM §8.3 Field Demonstrations
  • Supervised field demonstration at the operational ride for each CRC candidate
  • Observable competency criteria from ASTM F770-18 §8.3 assessed in real time
  • Evaluator signs field demo record for each candidate — pass/fail documented
  • Failed demonstrations remediated immediately — documented re-assessment
  • Complete field demo package included in pre-opening compliance file
  • Signed by AS evaluator and park supervisor of record
Deliverable 03
🚨
Emergency Drill Facilitation & Documentation
  • Full emergency scenario drill conducted at the ride — stuck-ride evacuation, medical response, fire/weather
  • AS facilitates the drill and evaluates response against the SOP
  • Gaps identified and documented with remediation recommendations
  • Drill report signed by AS facilitator and park operations lead
  • Drill documentation meets ASTM and state regulatory requirements
Deliverable 04
📂
Pre-Opening Compliance Package
  • Pre-opening inspection conducted and documented against ASTM §7.1
  • AS-signed pre-opening inspection attestation included in package
  • All CRC certifications, field demo records, and drill documentation compiled
  • Formatted under manufacturer letterhead — your brand on the cover
  • Delivered in physical and digital formats before opening day
  • Designed for retention — audit-ready, insurer-ready, litigation-ready
Optional Add-On

Opening Day Observation — AS remains on-site for the first public operating session, observing operator performance and providing real-time feedback. Any deviations from SOP are documented and a post-opening brief is delivered to park management. Available for $1,500 additional.

Engagement Timeline

How the 30-Day Window Works

The Intensive is designed to fit inside a park's installation and commissioning window — not add time to it.

−30
30 Days Before Opening
Scheduling & Pre-Work
AS confirms CRC candidate list with the park, verifies course completions in the portal, and coordinates on-site visit dates with park operations team. Pre-opening inspection criteria are confirmed against the ride-specific SOP.
D1
On-Site Day 1
Live Certification & Field Demonstrations
AS evaluator arrives on-site. Full CRC cohort completes final exam and ASTM §8.3 field demonstrations at the ride. Failed candidates are remediated. Signed certifications and field demo records issued same day.

On-Site Day 2
Emergency Drill & Pre-Opening Inspection
Emergency scenarios are run, evaluated, and documented. AS conducts and signs off on the pre-opening inspection per ASTM §7.1. Any operational gaps identified are escalated to the CRC and park management with documented remediation steps.

Compliance Package Delivery
Complete pre-opening compliance package compiled, formatted under manufacturer letterhead, and delivered to park and manufacturer. AS portal updated with all certification records. Park is documentation-ready to open.

AS evaluator remains on-site for first public operating session. Real-time performance observation, deviation documentation, and post-opening debrief with park management. Add-on: $1,500.
