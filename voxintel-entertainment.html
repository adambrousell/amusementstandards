The Post-Rust Reality

Paperwork documents what was planned.
VoxIntel documents what actually happened.

Criminal liability is now personal.

The Rust shooting and subsequent criminal prosecution fundamentally changed the liability landscape for film production. Producers, line producers, and safety coordinators now face personal criminal exposure for on-set safety failures. The industry responded with more paperwork — longer safety meetings, more forms, more signatures. But paperwork documents what was planned, not what actually happened. VoxIntel documents what actually happened, in real time, from the radio traffic that's already flowing.

Every film set runs on Motorola walkie-talkies. Hundreds of transmissions per hour flow across multiple channels — scene calls, safety checks, talent movements, stunt coordination. 100% of it disappears the moment it's spoken.

The Pipeline

From walkie traffic to
structured production intelligence.

A passive scanner sits near set. No integration with existing radios. No crew training. 30-minute setup. $170 in hardware.

Passive SDR receiver monitors all production walkie channels simultaneously from a single device near set.
