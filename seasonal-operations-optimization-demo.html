Accelerated Onboarding System Target: operational in 7 days
Day 1
Pre-arrival digital orientation
LMS modules assigned before first day. Safety policy, park overview, role fundamentals — completed on personal device. Saves 3–4 hours of Day 1 classroom time.
Day 2–3
Ride-specific certification
Attraction-specific modules with knowledge checks. Supervised shadow shifts on assigned ride. Certification checklist signed off by lead operator.
Day 4–5
Supervised solo operation
New hire operates independently with scheduled check-ins. Incident reporting procedures reviewed. Guest communication standards assessed.
Day 6–7
Certification sign-off + cross-training begins
Final ride certification completed. Cross-training on secondary attraction begins to increase schedule flexibility and reduce single-point-of-failure staffing.

Weather Contingency Playbook · 5 Scenarios
Pre-Built Weather Protocols Guest communication scripts · staff redeployment · refund policies
🌧️
Rain Protocol
Partial closure decision tree based on rainfall rate. Indoor redeployment assignments pre-mapped. Guest communication script and comp voucher policy ready to activate.
Trigger: >0.25in/hr
⚡
Lightning Protocol
Full outdoor shutdown procedure with ride evacuation sequence. 30-minute all-clear timer. Guest shelter capacity mapped across the park. Staff safety accountability checklist.
Trigger: Within 10 miles
🌡️
Heat Protocol
Staff rotation schedule shortens when heat index exceeds thresholds. Cooling stations activated. Guest hydration messaging deployed via PA and digital signage. Modified operating hours plan.
Trigger: Heat index >100°F
💨
Wind Protocol
Ride-specific wind speed thresholds mapped per manufacturer spec. Automated monitoring alerts lead operators. Ride-by-ride shutdown sequence minimizes guest disruption while maintaining safety compliance.
Trigger: Ride-specific mph
