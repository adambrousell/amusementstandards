The Three Core Modules

Run the park.
Not paperwork.

Three interconnected operational modules your management, dispatch, and ride operations teams use every shift. Real-time data flows between them — when an operator opens a ride in ATR, the Manager's clear-to-open board updates. When EAP roles are assigned in Manager, they push live to the Dispatcher. OpsScan, LensIQ, and VoxIntel feed all three modules with independent real-world data.

👔
MGR
Manager Module
The command center. Runs your entire shift from open to close — and houses 19 inspection and audit forms. OpsScan sign-off data and LensIQ throughput feed directly into the command dashboard.
  • Dashboard — live park status, OOS count, open CARs, critical rotations
  • RIDE Score — composite with four pillar breakdown
  • Out-of-Service tracking — mechanical, staffing, weather, inspection
  • Corrective Action pipeline — CAR creation, aging buckets, escalation
  • Inspections tab — 19 audit and inspection forms
  • Shift & Handoff — shift logs, handoff documents
  • Throughput logging — per-attraction guests, TPH, capacity % via LensIQ
  • Rotation management — warn/critical thresholds, OpsScan timer-driven alerts
  • Ops Panel dual sign-off — clears to open board via OpsScan
  • Escalation Contact Matrix — tiered contact directory
  • Emergency Response Role Cards — assign at shift start, push to OPD
  • VoxIntel Radio Intelligence — all-channel transcription, event classification, congestion scoring in command dashboard

📡
OPD
Ops Dispatcher
Real-time ride status board and operator assignment hub. The dispatcher's live view of everything on the floor — fed by OpsScan position data and VoxIntel radio intelligence.
  • Ride status board — open, down, hold, delayed per unit
  • Operator-to-unit assignments — who is where, from OpsScan scans
  • Rotation compliance — timer-driven with OpsScan position alerts
  • EAP role assignments — received live from Manager
  • Coverage gap alerts — understaffed positions flagged by OpsScan
  • VoxIntel Radio Intelligence — live multi-channel feed, NLP classification, step-on collision detection

ATR + OpsScan
At-Ride / Operator Log
The ride operator's daily tool — powered by OpsScan QR position scan-in. 90 seconds of interaction generates a full shift of structured risk data. No app download, no login required.
  • QR position scan-in — Load, Control, Unload, Queue positions
  • Ops Panel dual sign-off — Maintenance + Operations required before clear-to-open
  • Voice-to-incident — speak 5 seconds, AI structures the report
  • Rotation intelligence — dynamic reminders adjusted for heat, OLI, open CARs
  • Cycle count tap — TPH calculates automatically from dispatch taps
  • Anomaly detection — AI flags speed, zero-variance, and clustering patterns nightly
  • Auto CAR generation — every trigger event drafts a complete corrective action record

Data Acquisition Layer

Three independent signals.
One defensible score.

AS 360 ingests data from three independent sources that cannot be gamed simultaneously. When signals agree, confidence is high. When they diverge — that gap is the finding. No competitor can replicate this triangulation without building all three layers.

Layer 2 · Field
📱
OpsScan
QR-based operator position scan-in at every ride touchpoint. Voice incident reporting, AI anomaly detection, rotation intelligence, and auto CAR generation. 90-second interaction. Full shift data.
  • Position QR scan-in — Load, Control, Unload, Queue
  • Ops Panel dual sign-off — Maintenance + Operations
  • Voice-to-incident — Whisper + Claude AI pipeline
  • Behavioral anomaly detection — nightly AI batch
  • Dynamic rotation reminders — heat, OLI, CAR-aware
  • Works on any smartphone over 4G — no WiFi, no app

Layer 3 · Visual
👁️
LensIQ
Computer vision throughput tracking using your existing cameras. No new hardware. Claude Vision counts guests per cycle, calculates TPH, and flags anomalies — operator-independent.
  • Integrates with existing CCTV — Verkada, Hikvision, RTSP
  • Guest headcount per cycle — 95%+ accuracy at load platforms
  • Live TPH calculation — independent of operator self-reporting
  • Operator presence check — passive safety audit from camera
  • Throughput anomaly detection — silent slowdowns flagged
  • Zero video storage — structured count data only
View LensIQ →
Layer 1 · Passive
📡
VoxIntel
Passive RF monitoring of all venue radio channels. Real-time transcription, NLP event classification, ride-down detection, step-on collision detection, and congestion scoring — no operator involvement required.
  • All-channel radio capture — RTL-SDR hardware
  • Whisper transcription + Claude NLP classification
  • Ride-down detection — feeds OOS board automatically
  • Step-on collision detection — dispatcher safety alert
  • Channel congestion scoring — communication risk metric
  • Feeds MGR command dashboard + OPD live view
