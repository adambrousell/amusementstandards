AuditScore · Gamified Compliance Engine

Inspections that
drive themselves.

Operators earn XP for completing audits on time. Supervisors see team streaks. Managers build their own management streaks. The anomaly engine watches for fabricated compliance. Every audit feeds the RIDE Score Readiness pillar.

34s
vs. 4.1 min peer avg. Checklist completed faster than anyone can physically inspect.
ZERO-VARIANCE FLAG
14 days
100% PASS on every item. Statistically improbable across 22-item checklist.
CLUSTERING FLAG
90s
3 checklists before supervisor arrival. Batch-completion pattern.
MANAGER STREAK + PARK HEALTH INDEX
Consecutive shifts with complete handoffs, no missed inspections, all CARs addressed. 7/14/21-day streak badges in handoff docs. PHI = RIDE 35% + Inspection 25% + CACR 25% + Rotation 15%.
AUDIT AMENDMENT REQUEST
Original record sealed. Amendment geo-verified, device-confirmed, photo-evidenced. Supervisor approves within 4 hours. Both records visible at every access tier. Frequency is an Integrity pillar input.

For Specialty Lines Insurers

Risk quantified.
Not described.

AS 360 translates operational risk into a continuous scored index — the same function FICO serves in credit. Three independent data sources ensure the score cannot be gamed. Claims-to-score correlation validates the signal.

CLAIMS-TO-SCORE CORRELATION
STRONG (75+): 1.8 claims / 3yr. WATCH/CRITICAL (<60): 9.8 claims / 3yr. A 5.4× multiplier. Correlation strengthens as the dataset matures.
INSURER DATA OVERLAY (NDA)
Claims history, loss control reports, and loss runs ingested under mutual NDA. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, tenant-isolated. Your operators never see insurer data.
