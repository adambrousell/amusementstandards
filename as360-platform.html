The Three Core Modules

Run the park.
Not paperwork.

Three interconnected operational modules your management, dispatch, and ride operations teams use every shift. Real-time data flows between them — when an operator opens a ride in ATR, the Manager's clear-to-open board updates. When EAP roles are assigned in Manager, they push live to the Dispatcher.

👔
MGR
Manager Module
The command center. Runs your entire shift from open to close — and houses 19 inspection and audit forms in its Inspections tab.
  • Dashboard — live park status, OOS count, open CARs, critical rotations
  • RIDE Score — composite with four pillar breakdown
  • Out-of-Service tracking — mechanical, staffing, weather, inspection
  • Corrective Action pipeline — CAR creation, aging buckets, escalation
  • Inspections tab — 19 audit and inspection forms
  • Shift & Handoff — shift logs, handoff documents
  • Throughput logging — per-attraction guests, TPH, capacity %
  • Rotation management — warn/critical thresholds, timer-driven alerts
  • Escalation Contact Matrix — tiered contact directory
  • Emergency Response Role Cards — assign at shift start, push to OPD
  • Management Roster & Inspection Schedule
📡
OPD
Ops Dispatcher
Real-time ride status board and operator assignment hub. The dispatcher's live view of everything happening on the floor.
  • Ride status board — open, down, hold, delayed per unit
  • Operator-to-unit assignments — who is where, right now
  • Rotation compliance — timer-driven with alerts from MGR
  • Radio log book — timestamped communication record
  • EAP role assignments — received live from Manager
  • Coverage gap alerts — understaffed positions flagged
🎡
ATR
At-Ride / Operator Log
The ride operator's daily tool. Opening checklist, cycle logging, anomaly flags — synced live to Manager and Dispatcher.
  • Opening checklist — pre-open inspection per ride
  • Operator shift log — rotation reminders, cycle counts
  • Throughput linked to dispatch — guest counts, TPH
  • Units in service tracker — syncs to MGR clear-to-open
  • Restraint check logging — per-cycle documentation
  • Anomaly flags — immediate escalation to Manager
