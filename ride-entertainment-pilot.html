The Problem Worth Solving
Every Park Running a Ride You Sold, Installed, or Operate Is Using a Standard You Never Set
Ride Entertainment touches the full lifecycle — sales, installation, operation, financial partnerships. But at every stage, operator qualification is defined by the park, not by you or the manufacturer. The documentation gap exists across every division.

The Gap

Parks running Gerstlauer coasters, Funtime Star Flyers, and Skyline Skywarps are all defining "qualified operator" independently. No manufacturer standard exists. No RE standard exists. Every installation is a separate training island.

The Risk

Without a documented manufacturer or distributor verification standard, incident liability flows upstream — toward the people who sold, installed, and in some cases still operate the equipment. The paper trail currently stops at the sales contract.

The Advantage

No other company in the amusement industry spans sales, installation, operation, and financial partnerships under one roof. RE can offer verified operator programs across every manufacturer it represents — a new revenue layer on existing relationships.

The Combined Portfolio
