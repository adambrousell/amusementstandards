The Process

Three phases.
One score.

Amusement Standards deploys AS 360 SPLASH across your aquatic facility. Every data stream is independent — no single source controls the outcome. When streams agree, confidence is high. When they diverge, that gap is the finding.

01
🏗️

Deploy

Amusement Standards embeds AS 360 SPLASH across your aquatic operation. Audit schedules are set, water quality feeds are connected, and lifeguard zone mapping is documented. No disruption to daily operations or guest programming.

02
📡

Collect

Twelve independent streams feed the system simultaneously — in-house inspections, lifeguard zone audits, water chemistry readings, covert guest patrols, near-miss logs, and automated health department feeds. No single source controls the outcome.

03
📊

Score

All streams converge into six pillar scores — Safety, Protocol, Lifeguard, Aquatic, Structure, Hazard — which aggregate into a single composite SPLASH Score. Insurers receive a structured risk dossier. Operators receive a prioritized action list.

The Framework

Six pillars.
360° aquatic coverage.

Every stream feeds one or more pillars. Pillar scores are independent — a strong Safety score does not mask a weak Lifeguard score. The composite reflects all six dimensions simultaneously.

S
Safety

Active Prevention

Active incident prevention systems, emergency action plan currency, first aid station readiness, AED placement and certification, and guest safety communication — signage, PA coverage, and staff visibility throughout the aquatic footprint.

Protocol

Documented Compliance

SOP currency for slide cycles, capacity management, height enforcement, chemical handling, opening/closing procedures, and corrective action closure rates. The paper trail pillar — what your regulators and insurers will ask for first.

Contributing Streams
🔒 WEIGHTING CONFIDENTIAL · NDA REQUIRED
L
Lifeguard

Zone & Staff Performance

Certification currency (Ellis, StarGuard, Red Cross), rotation schedule adherence, scanning zone coverage maps, fatigue management compliance, in-service training frequency, and response time documentation. The highest liability exposure pillar in aquatic operations.

Contributing Streams
🔒 WEIGHTING CONFIDENTIAL · NDA REQUIRED
A
Aquatic

Water Quality & Chemistry

Water chemistry (pH, free chlorine, combined chlorine, turbidity, temperature), filtration system operational status, health department inspection history, recreational water illness (RWI) prevention protocols, and bather load management against permitted capacity.

Contributing Streams
🔒 WEIGHTING CONFIDENTIAL · NDA REQUIRED
S
Structure

Physical Plant Condition

Physical condition of slides, pools, decks, ladders, wave and flow systems, drainage infrastructure, and mechanical ride components. Inspection cadence, maintenance work order closure rates, and manufacturer service bulletin compliance status.

Contributing Streams
🔒 WEIGHTING CONFIDENTIAL · NDA REQUIRED
H
Hazard

Environmental Risk

Weather response protocols and lightning detection compliance, Virginia Graeme Baker Act drain entrapment compliance, slip/fall surface condition audits, chemical storage and handling safety, UV exposure management, and crowd density monitoring at peak load.
