Who This Is For

Safety Culture Accelerator is for teams who know that procedures alone aren’t enough. If you're seeing inconsistency between what’s written and what’s done, or if near‑misses aren’t being surfaced, this program is built for you.

What’s Included

  • Staff interviews and on‑the‑ground observations
  • Leadership alignment workshop
  • Safety Culture Scorecard
  • Training and communication recommendations
  • 90‑day improvement roadmap

Outcomes

  • Better alignment between leadership, supervisors, and frontline staff
  • Clearer expectations and more consistent follow‑through
  • Improved reporting of issues, near‑misses, and concerns
  • A practical plan to move culture from “compliance” to “ownership”

Engagement Format

Duration: Flat‑rate, ~30‑day engagement

Delivery: Mix of remote sessions and optional on‑site visit

Deliverables: Culture scorecard, workshop materials, and 90‑day roadmap
