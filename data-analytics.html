What we actually build

Our analytics outputs are built in R, structured for defensibility, and designed to communicate clearly to both operators and underwriters.

HEATMAP
Attraction-Level Risk Heatmaps
Visual risk concentration maps plotted by attraction type, zone, time of day, and incident severity. Immediately shows where exposure is concentrating — and where it's been hiding.
TREND
Temporal Trend Modeling
Time-series analysis of incident frequency and severity across seasons, staffing cycles, and operational changes. Distinguishes signal from noise and surfaces statistically meaningful patterns.
ROOT
Root Cause Clustering
Natural language classification and clustering of incident narratives to identify recurring causal categories — human factors, mechanical, environmental, procedural — across hundreds of records.
PREDICT
Predictive Risk Indicators
Leading indicator modeling that identifies operational and behavioral signals associated with elevated incident likelihood before incidents occur — not after.
BENCH
Cross-Location Benchmarking
For multi-property operators: normalized comparison of incident rates, severity distributions, and documentation quality across locations — with ranking and triage prioritization.
INSURER
Insurer-Ready Packaging
Every deliverable formatted for broker and underwriter review — structured narratives, clean visualizations, and corrective action evidence designed to support premium defense and coverage negotiations.
