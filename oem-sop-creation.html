What OEM SOP Creation Is

One Document. Two Critical Jobs.

Most ride manufacturers provide technical specifications, maintenance manuals, and engineering drawings. Almost none provide a park-ready Standard Operating Procedure — the document that translates your engineering into daily operational practice.

Amusement Standards bridges that gap. We take your OEM technical documentation and author a complete, structured SOP that a park can hand to their ride team and a regulator can review on-site.

Why This Matters for CRC

The CRC certification course is only as defensible as the source document it's built from. An AS-authored SOP grounded in your OEM documentation creates an unbroken paper trail: manufacturer spec → SOP → CRC course → certified operator. That chain doesn't exist without this document.

Document Structure

Every Section Built to ASTM F770-18

The SOP is structured to mirror ASTM F770-18 section requirements so that compliance mapping is explicit and auditable at every page.

Section 01
Ride Overview & Systems Description
Ride type, capacity, cycle time, key mechanical and electrical systems, control panel layout, and manufacturer specifications. Written for operational staff, not engineers.
ASTM §6 — General
Section 02
Pre-Opening Inspection Protocol
Step-by-step daily inspection checklist covering mechanical, electrical, and safety systems. Formatted as a checkable field document with pass/fail criteria and escalation procedures.
ASTM §7.1 — Pre-Opening
Section 03
Operator Qualification Standards
Minimum qualifications, training requirements, competency demonstration criteria, and the documentation framework for operator qualification records.
ASTM §8.1 — Qualifications
Section 04
Operational Duties & Ride Cycle
Full ride cycle procedure — rider screening, height and health restrictions, restraint verification, dispatch, monitoring, unload. Every step enumerated with decision criteria.
ASTM §8.2 — Duties
Section 05
Emergency Procedures
E-stop locations and activation, ride evacuation protocols, medical emergency response, fire and weather procedures, and incident command structure during an active event.
ASTM §8.2.4 — Emergency
Section 06
LOTO & Maintenance Handoff
Lockout/tagout procedure for operational staff, maintenance notification protocols, and the documentation required before and after any maintenance activity affecting ride operation.
ASTM §7.3 — Maintenance
Section 07
Field Demonstration Requirements
Competency demonstration criteria for CRC certification — specific observable behaviors, pass/fail standards, and the documentation format for field demo sign-off.
ASTM §8.3 — Field Demo
Section 08
Incident Reporting & Documentation
Incident classification, initial response documentation requirements, notification chain, and record retention standards aligned with CPSC and state reporting requirements.
ASTM §10 — Incidents
Section 09
Signage & Guest Communication Standards
Required signage by ride zone, height and health restriction language, guest communication scripts for common scenarios, and queue management standards.
ASTM §9 — Signage
