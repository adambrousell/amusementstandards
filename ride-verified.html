How RIDE-Verified Works

Step 1: Platform Setup (Week 1)

We configure your mobile audit platform with pre-built templates for your attraction types, set up user accounts, and train your supervisors on daily/weekly self-audit workflows.

Step 2: Continuous Self-Auditing (Ongoing)

Your team conducts regular self-audits using the mobile app—daily opening inspections, weekly safety walkthroughs, monthly compliance checks. All results flow to your dashboard.

Step 3: Quarterly Third-Party Audits (Unannounced)

Our certified auditors arrive unannounced, observe operations using the same mobile platform, score performance across 40+ criteria, and deliver video-documented reports within 48 hours.

Step 4: Continuous Improvement (Monthly/Quarterly)

Your compliance advisor reviews audit results, identifies trends, provides corrective action guidance, and tracks progress toward operational excellence goals.

Step 5: Annual Performance Review

Comprehensive year-end report summarizing all audits, incidents, corrective actions, and performance trends—formatted for insurance renewals and regulatory documentation.
