Incident & Risk Management

A complete, defensible program for stronger reporting and clearer operational standards.

Request This Program

Who This Is For

This program is built for parks, FECs, waterparks, and event operators who need consistent, defensible reporting and clearer visibility into operational risk. If your incident data is inconsistent, incomplete, or difficult to analyze, this gives you a structured foundation.

What's Included

  • Injury Classification System (types, severity levels, categories)
  • Near Miss Reporting Workflows with QR‑code mobile forms
  • Root Cause Analysis Tools (5 Whys, fishbone, human‑factors checklists)
  • Corrective Action Tracking with verification steps
  • Monthly Trend Reports (patterns, heat maps, severity trends)
  • Annual Safety Performance Summary

Tiered Program Options

  • Tier 1 — Incident Reporting Essentials: $6,500–$8,500 setup + $350–$550/month
  • Tier 2 — Full Incident & Risk Management System: $18,000–$25,000 setup + $750–$1,250/month
  • Tier 3 — Continuous Improvement Program: $30,000–$40,000/year

Outcomes

  • Cleaner, more consistent incident data
  • Defensible documentation for insurers and regulators
  • Clearer visibility into trends and root causes
  • Alignment between reporting, corrective actions, and leadership expectations

Engagement Format

Delivery: System build + ongoing analytics

Support: Monthly reporting, data integrity checks, and supervisor support

Optional: On‑site training, insurer presentations, and SOP alignment
