Incident & Risk Management Standards

Data-driven systems to identify trends, reduce risk, and demonstrate continuous improvement.

Built for parks, FECs, waterparks, and event operators who need consistent reporting and clear visibility into operational risk.

Overview

Our Incident & Risk Management Standards program gives your operation a complete, standardized, and defensible system for documenting incidents, analyzing root causes, tracking corrective actions, and demonstrating continuous improvement to insurers, regulators, and stakeholders.

This is a turnkey, data-driven framework designed specifically for amusement and attractions operations where safety, documentation quality, and trend visibility directly impact liability, guest experience, and long-term performance.

What You Get

Injury Classification System

A standardized framework that ensures every incident is categorized consistently across teams, locations, and seasons. This creates clean, comparable data that supports internal reviews and external stakeholders.

  • Injury types
  • Severity levels
  • Event categories
  • OSHA-aligned terminology
  • Definitions & examples for supervisors

Near Miss Reporting Workflows

Simple, mobile-friendly workflows that make it easy for staff to report hazards before they become incidents, increasing visibility into operational blind spots.

  • QR-code mobile forms
  • Simple, guided reporting steps
  • Escalation triggers for higher-risk events
  • Supervisor review and follow-up processes
  • Data validation rules for consistent entries

Root Cause Analysis Templates

Structured tools that help supervisors and managers identify the true underlying causes of incidents, not just the surface-level symptoms.

  • 5 Why templates
  • Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagrams
  • Human-factors checklists
  • Corrective-action forms linked to findings

Corrective Action Tracking

A clear, accountable system that prevents issues from falling through the cracks and ensures that corrective actions are assigned, completed, and verified.

  • Assignment workflows with responsible parties
  • Due dates and reminders
  • Verification and sign-off steps
  • Closure documentation for each action
  • Accountability dashboards for leadership

Mobile-Friendly Reporting Tools

Fast, accurate reporting from anywhere in the facility, even during peak operations, with tools that fit naturally into your team’s daily workflow.

  • QR-based access to incident and near-miss forms
  • Guided digital workflows for staff and supervisors
  • Photo, witness, and location capture
  • Auto-routed notifications to the right leaders

Monthly Trend Reports

Delivered monthly with clear, visual summaries that help leadership understand what is changing and where to focus.

  • Incident counts by type, location, and time period
  • Severity trends and emerging patterns
  • Root-cause patterns and contributing factors
  • Heat maps for higher-risk areas or activities
  • Corrective-action status and follow-through

Annual Safety Performance Summary

A comprehensive year-end analysis that demonstrates continuous improvement and supports insurance and regulatory conversations.

  • Year-over-year comparisons
  • Reduction metrics and improvement highlights
  • Corrective-action effectiveness review
  • Recommendations and priorities for the next year
