Guest Experience Performance Program

Your foundation for more consistent, higher‑performing guest service.

Request This Program

Who This Is For

The Guest Experience Performance Program is designed for parks, FECs, and attractions that know guest service is inconsistent across shifts, teams, or locations—but don't have a clear, objective way to measure and improve it.

If you suspect:

  • Some teams deliver great service while others just "get through the day"
  • Queue management, greetings, and service recovery vary by person
  • Cleanliness, courtesy, and communication aren't consistently enforced
  • You're leaving positive reviews, repeat visits, and per‑cap spending on the table

…this program gives you a clear, actionable baseline for guest experience performance.

What's Included

We combine on‑site or remote observations, mobile audits, and structured standards to give you a practical, real‑world picture of how your guest experience actually performs.

📋

Guest Service Standards Review

Alignment of your current expectations (if they exist) with what's actually happening on the ground across shifts and teams.

📱

Mobile Guest Service Audits

Secret‑shop style observations using digital checklists focused on greetings, courtesy, communication, and service recovery.

Try the audit checklist Live Demo
🚶

Queue & Guest Flow Assessment

How guests move, wait, and interact with staff in high‑friction areas — entrances, queues, food service, and key touchpoints.

✨

Cleanliness & Presentation Review

Front‑of‑house appearance, signage clarity, wayfinding, and first‑impression readiness across all guest‑facing areas.

🔄

Service Recovery Snapshot

How staff respond when something goes wrong — delays, complaints, confusion, or guest frustration. Evaluated against the HEARD framework.

🎯

Prioritized Recommendations

Clear, ranked actions: quick wins, behavior standards, and structural fixes that improve guest experience fast.

Outcomes

By the end of the engagement, you'll have:

  • 1

    A clear view of your guest experience strengths and gaps

    Not just "we need better service," but specific behaviors, locations, and patterns backed by structured observations.

  • 2

    Defined, observable guest service standards

    What "good" looks like for greetings, communication, queue management, and service recovery — written down and measurable.

  • 3

    Immediate, practical steps your team can implement

    Quick wins for frontline staff, supervisors, and leadership that create visible improvement within weeks.

  • 4

    Documentation you can share with leadership and marketing

    A concrete story about how you're improving guest experience — not just safety — with data to back it up.

  • 5

    A foundation for training and LMS content

    Real behaviors and scenarios from your operation that can be turned into targeted training modules in TalentLMS or any platform.
