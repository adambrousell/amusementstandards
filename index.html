About Amusement Standards

We look at your operation the way an insurer, a regulator, or an attorney would

With more than 20 years of hands-on experience in ride operations, safety management, regulatory compliance, and data analytics, Amusement Standards works with amusement parks, FECs, water parks, traveling carnivals, adventure parks, trampoline parks, seasonal attractions, and municipal recreation departments across the country.

We don't hand off a report and disappear. We stay with you — through implementation, training, verification, and ongoing support. The longer you work with us, the stronger your safety program gets.

  • We stay involved: From the first assessment through implementation, not just delivery.
  • Cross-disciplinary team: Operations + analytics + regulatory + legal — all under one roof.
  • Built for your insurer: Every program designed to strengthen underwriting confidence.
  • Data-backed recommendations: Not opinions — evidence.
  • Documentation that holds up: In an audit, a claim, or a courtroom.
  • Works at any scale: From a single carnival unit to a multi-park portfolio.
