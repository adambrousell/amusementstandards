Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with us

What types of facilities do you work with?
+
We work with amusement parks, water parks, FECs, trampoline parks, traveling carnivals, county fairs, adventure parks, boardwalk venues, municipal recreation departments, go-kart tracks, and ride manufacturers. If guests interact with rides, attractions, or aquatic features at your facility, we can help.
What is the AS 360 platform?
+
AS 360 is our scoring platform. It takes the data from everything we do with you — training completions, audit results, incident reports — and turns it into a clear safety score. Think of it like a credit score for your operation's safety posture. The score shows where you're strong, where you're exposed, and improves over time as you address findings. You can learn the full details on our AS 360 Platform page.
What are the RIDE, SPLASH, and PLAY Scores?
+
They're three separate safety scores for three types of operations. RIDE Score is for dry ride environments (coasters, flats, dark rides). SPLASH Score is for aquatic venues (water parks, pools, splash pads). PLAY Score is for FECs (go-karts, trampolines, laser tag, arcades). Each one works on its own. If you run a hybrid facility, they combine into a single Park Intelligence Composite.
How quickly can you respond to a serious incident?
+
Our Emergency Incident Response program deploys within 48 hours. We provide rapid expert investigation and defensible documentation — the kind that insurance adjusters, attorneys, and regulators need to see.
What does the free risk review include?
+
It's a 15-minute phone call. We learn about your operation — what you run, how many staff you have, what your insurer or regulator is asking for — and we tell you where we think your biggest gaps are and which AS 360 score fits and what a first assessment would cover. No cost, no obligation.
Do we need the AS 360 platform to work with you?
+
AS 360 is the methodology behind every engagement. You can start with a single Facility Score Certificate (one-time, flat-rate) without committing to ongoing scoring — but the assessment itself is always AS 360-based.
Do you work with insurance carriers?
+
Yes. Our Pre-Bind Risk Assessment program gives underwriters a scored facility report — AS 360 Facility Score Certificate, pillar-level breakdowns, key findings, and pre-bind recommendations — in as fast as 48 hours. We also offer portfolio-level monitoring for carriers managing multiple insured facilities.
