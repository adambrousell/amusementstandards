About Amusement Standards

With more than 20 years of experience in safety consulting, risk management, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence, Amusement Standards has become a trusted partner for amusement parks, FECs, water parks, adventure parks, trampoline parks, seasonal attractions, and municipal recreation departments across the country. Our philosophy is simple: we don’t just hand off recommendations — we stay with you through implementation, training, verification, and ongoing support. Our team brings together a rare combination of operational expertise, data analytics, regulatory insight, and forensic-level documentation standards. This multidisciplinary approach allows us to evaluate your operation the same way insurers, regulators, and litigators do — and then help you build systems that exceed those expectations.

Our Leadership Team

Matt Smith — Senior Safety & Operations Consultant
With a career spanning more than two decades in the amusement and attractions industry, Matt has led safety programs, operational teams, and multi-site risk management initiatives across several states. His background includes hands-on leadership in ride operations, training program development, emergency response planning, and regulatory compliance. Matt is known for his ability to translate complex safety requirements into practical, staff-friendly systems that improve both guest experience and operational reliability. He has supported facilities through state inspections, insurance audits, incident investigations, and major operational transitions — always with a focus on clarity, consistency, and defensibility.

Adam Brousell — Director of Data Analytics & Risk Intelligence
Adam brings deep expertise in data analysis, claims trend modeling, and operational risk forecasting. With a background in analytics and safety program evaluation, he specializes in transforming raw incident data, loss runs, and operational metrics into actionable insights that drive measurable risk reduction. His work includes developing injury trend dashboards, predictive risk models, attraction level heat maps, and insurer-ready analytics packages. Adam’s analytical frameworks help operators understand not just what is happening, but why — and what to do next. His ability to merge data science with real-world operations gives clients a competitive advantage during insurance renewals and safety planning.

Landan Ansell, CPA, CFF, J.D. — Regulatory & Compliance Advisor
Landan brings a unique combination of legal, financial, and forensic expertise to Amusement Standards. As a Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Certified in Financial Forensics (CFF), and a licensed attorney (J.D.), he provides high-level guidance on regulatory compliance, documentation defensibility, and risk governance. His background includes regulatory interpretation, policy development, financial impact analysis, and expert-level documentation review. Landan ensures that every standard, SOP, waiver, and safety system aligns with the expectations of regulators, insurers, auditors, and — when necessary — legal scrutiny. His involvement strengthens the defensibility and credibility of every program we deliver.
