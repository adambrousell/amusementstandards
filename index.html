About Amusement Standards

We evaluate your operation the way insurers, regulators, and litigators do

With more than 20 years of experience in safety consulting, risk management, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence, Amusement Standards has become a trusted partner for amusement parks, FECs, water parks, adventure parks, trampoline parks, seasonal attractions, and municipal recreation departments across the country.

Our philosophy is simple: we don't just hand off recommendations — we stay with you through implementation, training, verification, and ongoing support.

  • Hands-on partnership: We stay involved from assessment through implementation, not just delivery.
  • Cross-disciplinary expertise: Operations + analytics + regulatory + legal — all under one roof.
  • Insurer-aligned systems: Every program built to strengthen underwriting confidence.
  • Data-driven decisions: Every recommendation backed by evidence and analytical rigor.
  • Defensible documentation: Designed to withstand audits, claims, and litigation.
  • Scalable support: From essential compliance tune-ups to full managed safety services.
