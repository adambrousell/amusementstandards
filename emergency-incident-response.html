Our Investigation Process

Phase 1: Initial Response (0-4 hours)

What happens:

  • You call our emergency hotline
  • 15-30 minute intake call to gather incident details
  • Lead investigator assigned based on case complexity
  • On-site visit or remote investigation scheduled

Phase 2: Investigation (4-48 hours)

Evidence we collect:

  • Ride operation logs & maintenance records
  • Operator training documentation
  • Video footage (surveillance & guest cell phones)
  • Photos of scene, equipment, and conditions
  • Weather data (if applicable)

Interviews we conduct:

  • Operator(s) on duty at time of incident
  • Supervisors and managers
  • Staff witnesses
  • Guest witnesses (when available)

Phase 3: Analysis & Reporting (48 hours - 5 days)

Our analysis includes:

  • Root cause determination (human, equipment, environmental, procedural)
  • Legal exposure assessment & negligence evaluation
  • Regulatory compliance status review
  • Industry standard comparison (ASTM, state regulations)

You receive:

  • Executive summary (1-2 pages)
  • Detailed incident timeline
  • Root cause analysis with contributing factors
  • Corrective action recommendations (immediate + long-term)
  • Documentation package for legal counsel/insurers

Phase 4: Follow-Up (30 days)

Ongoing support:

  • 30-day check-in call to review progress
  • Verification of corrective actions implemented
  • Additional guidance as needed
