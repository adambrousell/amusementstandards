Root Cause Patterns & Analyst Findings
Key Patterns Identified4 findings requiring action
01
Summer surge concentrated on two attractions
June–August accounts for 61% of all high-severity incidents. The Main Coaster and Log Flume generate 43% of these alone — both staffed by seasonal operators with under 30 days tenure at time of incident.
Operator Training Gap
02
Documentation completeness drops at shift change
38% of incidents recorded between 2–4pm show incomplete documentation. Cross-referencing with scheduling data shows this aligns with supervisor overlap — reports deferred and never completed.
Documentation Process
03
Repeat incident profiles on Drop Tower
14 of 31 Drop Tower incidents share near-identical root cause descriptions. Same procedure step referenced in 11 separate reports — a systemic SOP gap, not isolated operator error.
SOP Review Required
04
Insurer-reportable incidents under-classified
8–12 incidents that meet reportable thresholds were classified below reportable level. Creates retroactive liability exposure if records are reviewed during a claim.
Compliance Risk
