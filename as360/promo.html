AUDITSCORE · GAMIFIED COMPLIANCE ENGINE
Inspections that drive themselves.
AuditScore turns your existing pre-open inspection schedule into a continuous behavioral dataset. Operators earn XP for completing audits on time and thoroughly. The data feeds the Readiness pillar — not just a paper record.
XP + Streak Engine
Operators build XP and streaks from daily inspection completion. Consecutive shift completions multiply. Missed audits break streaks.
