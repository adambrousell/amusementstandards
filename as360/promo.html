SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
How the streams converge
The RIDE Score is built from 16 independent data streams across four categories: audit data (in-house GSA, mystery shopper, safety inspections), incident & compliance data (near-miss reports, incident records, corrective action closure rate), guest intelligence (QR feedback, post-visit surveys, online reviews), and operational data (dispatch logs, at-ride records, queue times, weather). No single stream controls a pillar — when sources agree, confidence is high. When they diverge, that gap is the finding. Hover any node to learn more. Click to open its form.

A single index representing total operational stress. High OLI with strong audits = well-managed load. High OLI with weak audits = hidden risk. Divergence between OLI and pillar scores triggers a Convergence Alert.

Convergence Confidence
OLI's confidence score rises as more independent streams corroborate the same load reading. Single-source agreement yields low confidence. When 6+ streams converge on the same signal, OLI achieves high confidence and feeds the composite RIDE Score at full weight.
