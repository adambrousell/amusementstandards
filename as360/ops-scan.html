How It Works

Scan In. The System
Does The Rest.

Every data point originates from an action the operator was already performing. No new behavior. No new paperwork.

01
Operator Arrives
Scans position QR with personal phone. No app, no login. Session opens instantly over 4G.
02
Shift Logged
Scan timestamp, operator ID, and position auto-populate the shift record in real time.
03
AI Monitors
Rotation reminders, anomaly detection, and OLI calculations run passively from scan data.
04
Issue Flagged
Operator holds mic, speaks 5 seconds. AI structures, classifies, and routes the report.
05
CAR Auto-Drafted
Complete corrective action record generated instantly. Supervisor approves with one tap.

AI Processing Layer

Behavioral Data.
Not Self-Reported.

The difference between a checklist tool and a risk intelligence platform is what happens when no one is watching. OpsScan's AI layer detects patterns no supervisor can monitor manually.

  • 01
    Speed Anomaly
    Pre-open median is 4.2 minutes. Sub-60-second submission triggers an Integrity pillar flag — possible incomplete inspection.
  • 02
    Zero-Variance Pattern
    30 consecutive days of 100% pass rates is statistically improbable. Auto-flagged without anyone needing to notice.
  • 03
    Clustering Detection
    Submissions clustering before supervisor arrival indicate compliance theater. Temporal correlation catches it passively.
  • 04
    Coverage Gap Alert
    Position unscanned 67 minutes on a 94°F day triggers an operator welfare check — not a report, an immediate supervisor SMS.
