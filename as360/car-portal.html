Multi-Layer Review Process

Five stages.
Nothing closes without verification.

Every CAR travels a defined path from field submission to verified closure. No stage can be skipped. Critical findings trigger automatic escalation. Third-party inspectors have a dedicated credentialed access layer — their sign-off is weighted differently from internal verification.

01
Field Submission
Operators · Maintenance · Inspectors
Submit Finding
Any credentialed user submits a CAR via OpsScan QR scan, portal form, or third-party inspector upload. Auto-generates CAR ID, timestamps submission, and routes to Operations Manager queue.
CAR ID auto-generated Ride / attraction linked Severity assigned Finding source tagged Photo/doc attach
XP awarded on valid submission · +50–150 pts depending on severity
02
Manager Review
Operations Manager
Acknowledge & Assign
Operations Manager reviews the finding, confirms severity, assigns an owner, sets a due date, and pushes to the assigned party's queue. Critical CARs trigger immediate Slack/email alert.
Severity confirm / override Owner assignment Due date set Priority flag Escalation trigger
SLA clock starts on acknowledgment · CRITICAL: 2hr ack SLA
03
Remediation
Assigned Owner
Document the Fix
Assigned party documents remediation steps, parts used, time spent, and attaches evidence (photo, test result, cert). Submits for verification — cannot self-close.
Remediation notes Parts / labor log Evidence upload NDT result attach Submit for verify
Speed bonus XP available · +100 pts for on-time closure
04
Independent Verification
Maintenance Manager · 3rd-Party Inspector
Verify Closure
A separate credentialed verifier confirms the fix is complete and adequate. Third-party inspectors carry a higher verification weight in the CACR calculation. The submitter cannot be the verifier.
Physical inspection confirm Evidence reviewed Adequate / Inadequate 3rd-party weight +1.4× Return to stage 3 if inadequate
Third-party sign-off feeds inspector credential score · NAARSO-adjacent ranking
05
Closure & Scoring
Operations Manager · AS 360
Close & Score
Manager closes the CAR. Full audit trail locked. CACR updates. The closure feeds back into the RIDE / SPLASH / PLAY Integrity pillar score — a closed CAR is a positive signal. An aged open CAR is a penalty.
Audit trail locked CACR recalculated Integrity pillar updated XP awarded to all parties Insurer export flagged
Full provenance visible to insurer · Source → Finding → Fix → Verifier chain

Review Board Structure

Every role.
Defined authority. Defined accountability.

The review board is a multi-role structure where authority is tiered by credentialing and independence. Operators surface findings. Managers assign and close. Verifiers confirm independently. Third-party inspectors carry elevated weight. No one closes their own finding.

Ride Operator / Field Staff
Surface & Submit
Submit new CAR from any attraction via OpsScan QR or portal
Attach photo evidence, voice note (VoxIntel integration)
Earn submission XP — rewarded for surfacing, never penalized
Cannot assign, verify, or close their own submission
Operations Manager
Review, Assign & Close
Acknowledge all incoming CARs · override severity if warranted
Assign to owner — maintenance, staff, or third-party inspector
Final closure authority after verification is complete
CACR dashboard visible in Manager Module live
Maintenance Manager
Remediate & Document
Primary remediation owner for mechanical, electrical, structural findings
Documents fix: parts, labor, NDT results, test records
Can verify CARs they did not remediate (cross-verification)
NDT upload center and inspector credential registry
Third-Party Inspector
Independent Verification
Credentialed access layer — NAARSO/AIMS cert number on file
Verification carries 1.4× weight in CACR calculation vs internal
Can submit CARs independently from on-site inspections
Builds inspector rank score — feed to credential leaderboard
Gamification Layer

Reward the operators
who surface problems.

The CAR system only works if people submit honestly. Gamification inverts the incentive — operators are rewarded for finding and reporting issues, not penalized. Speed bonuses reward fast remediation. Streaks recognize consistently clean operations. Inspector ranks build professional credentialing.

⚡
Submission XP
Points awarded on every valid CAR submission. Higher severity = more XP. The system rewards operators for being honest about what they find — not for having a clean record.
Critical: +150 XP · Major: +100 XP
Minor: +50 XP · Observation: +25 XP
Invalid / duplicate: 0 XP
🏁
Speed Bonus
Extra XP for closing CARs before the SLA deadline. The faster the remediation, the bigger the bonus. Creates a healthy competition between maintenance teams.
<50% of SLA time: +100 XP
50–80% of SLA: +50 XP
On time: Base XP only
Overdue: −25 XP
🔥
Clean Audit Streak
Consecutive weeks with zero overdue CARs and CACR above 90% earns a streak multiplier. Streaks are displayed on the facility leaderboard — visible to operators and insurers.
4-week streak: 1.25× XP multiplier
8-week streak: 1.5× multiplier
12-week streak: 🏆 Gold Tier badge
Broken streak: Reset to 1×
🏅
Inspector Rank
Third-party inspectors earn a rank score based on verification volume, accuracy, and speed. Builds toward an AS 360 Inspector credential that complements NAARSO and AIMS.
Level 1: 0–500 verifications
Level 2: 500–2,000 · +1.2× weight
Level 3: 2,000+ · +1.4× weight
Elite: 95%+ accuracy · Gold badge
📊
Facility Leaderboard
CACR rankings across all enrolled facilities. Operators know where they stand. Parks with high CACR scores get premium leverage at insurer renewal. Drives competition between facilities.
Public: Rank, CACR %, streak
Insurer-visible: Full CAR history
Operator-visible: Own scores + rank
Updated: Real-time
