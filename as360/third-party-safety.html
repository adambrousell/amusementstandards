Operator Positioning & Conduct

🎢 Load / Unload Area

Load / Unload Observation

Score these items at the boarding / unload area. You are now visible to operators.

0 of 14 items scored
Guest Screening & Loading
Height requirement is physically checked and enforced
Rider policies are communicated and enforced (health restrictions, loose articles, etc.)
Loading and unloading assistance provided where applicable
Rider posture and seating verified by operator before dispatch
Restraints physically tested by operator on each rider
5-second pre-dispatch visual scan performed before initiating ride
Communication & Staffing
Safety spiel delivered clearly and completely to each load group
No dispatch occurs while guests remain in restricted/unsafe areas
Operator staffing level is appropriate for this attraction type
OLI — Staffing Adequacy & Pressure Indicators
