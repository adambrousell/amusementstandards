The Concept

What is the AS 360 Platform?

AS 360 is the risk intelligence infrastructure for the U.S. attractions industry — the FICO score for operational safety. It delivers three independent, continuous composite indices, each purpose-built for a distinct venue type. Every score normalizes to 0–100 and is constructed from the same foundational methodology: multiple independent data streams, weighted by actuarial signal strength, converging into a single defensible number.

When all three scores are active at the same facility, they converge into the Park Intelligence Composite (PIC) — the industry's first whole-park risk index.

Cross-Score Intelligence

How the scores inform each other

In hybrid parks, each score contributes unique intelligence that calibrates the others.

RIDE→PLAY
Dispatch compliance trends from RIDE calibrate the Attraction pillar baseline in PLAY — weak dispatch culture on coasters almost always shows the same pattern on go-karts.
SPLASH→RIDE
SPLASH's EAP currency and first-aid readiness surface into RIDE's Readiness pillar — a park that drills its aquatic EAP is more prepared for ride emergencies too.
PLAY→SPLASH
PLAY's Youth pillar data — unaccompanied minor protocols, age enforcement — informs SPLASH's Lifeguard pillar weighting where the aquatic zone is a primary child-patron environment.

Scoring Math

How scores are normalized

All raw inputs across RIDE, SPLASH, and PLAY are converted to a 0–100 scale using one of three normalization methods:

Likert (1–5) fields use (score − 1) ÷ 4 × 100. Individual items carry explicit weights reflecting safety criticality — emergency contact knowledge is weighted 3× while nearest-ATM knowledge is weighted 1×.

Binary (YES/NO) fields use a pass rate: YES ÷ (YES + NO) × 100. N/A responses are excluded from the denominator.

Rate-based streams (NMR, IRL, OOS) use a penalty-decay model: 100 minus the event rate multiplied by a severity scalar. IRL adds a secondary severity-weighted penalty from the 1–5 severity scale.

Platform

Technical Infrastructure

Data is collected via web-based field tools (iGSA, mGSA, Operator Log, Operations Dispatcher) that write to Airtable. Power BI connects via the Airtable Web API connector and processes all scoring through 384+ DAX measures organized across 27 sections. Three external API feeds (QTX, WX, REV) provide environmental context. The architecture is designed for zero manual data entry between collection and dashboard — every score computes live from source data.
