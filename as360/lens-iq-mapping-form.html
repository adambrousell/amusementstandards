Where to find Nola IDs
Log into app.nolahq.com → navigate to your venue → the URL contains your LOCATION_ID. Call GET /api/v1/companies/{COMPANY_ID}/categories to get your CATEGORY_ID for throughput metrics. Contact hello@nolahq.com to get your COMPANY_ID and API credentials if not already set up.
Where to find Safari AI IDs
Contact your Safari AI account rep to get your LOCATION_ID and ZONE_ID values from their dashboard. Safari AI pushes data to your webhook endpoint — set your Worker URL as the destination: https://lensiq-ingest.amusementstandards.workers.dev/webhook/safari
Where to find SenSource IDs
Log into vea.sensourceinc.com → Locations → select your site → the SITE_ID appears in the URL. Your API CLIENT_ID and CLIENT_SECRET are in Settings → Integrations. SenSource API docs: vea.sensourceinc.com/api-docs
