Discussion-based · 60–90 min · No alarms · Tests decision-making · Log after each session
Scenario 1 — Multi-Casualty Incident
Tour bus accident · Advanced · 90 min · 8–12 participants
▼
Objectives: Mass casualty triage · ICS structure · Resource coordination · Media management · Family reunification
Phase 1 — Immediate Response (0–5 min)
INJECT
55-passenger tour bus rolled onto its side in parking lot. ~45 people, includes children from church camp. Multiple injuries, some still in bus.
DECISION POINT
Who do you call first? Who is IC? Do you go to the scene? How do you triage? How many ambulances?
Phase 2 — Expanding Incident
INJECT
News helicopter overhead. Reporter at main gate. Social media photos. Parents of church group arriving, demanding info about their children.
DECISION POINT
Who talks to media? Where do families wait? What info can you share? How do you reunite families with injured kids at hospital?
Phase 3 — Recovery
INJECT
8 transported (3 critical). Scene blocks main entrance. Monday: attorneys calling, OSHA at your door.
DECISION POINT
Can you stay open? Who handles attorney calls? What do you tell OSHA?

Scenario 2 — Active Threat
Workplace violence with social media · Advanced · 90 min
▼
Objectives: RUN-HIDE-FIGHT · Lockdown procedures · Law enforcement coordination · Employee trauma support
Phase 1 — Initial Reports
INJECT
Security radios: guest reports sounds like gunshots near Isle of Berk. Guests running. Social media posts showing panic. No confirmed shooter or injuries yet.
DECISION POINT
Lock down immediately or investigate? What do you announce to guests? Who calls 911? Clear all 5 worlds or just the area?
Phase 2 — Confirmed Threat
INJECT
Police confirm active threat. Suspect now in Dark Universe. Facility-wide lockdown required. 4,500 guests, 150+ staff. Staff radioing: "what do we do?"
DECISION POINT
How do you shelter 4,500 guests? PA announcement script? How do ride operators handle stranded guests? Corporate calling.
Phase 3 — Aftermath
INJECT
Threat neutralized. 3 injuries (not firearm). Media wants interview. Legal calling. Staff visibly shaken. Park wants to reopen tomorrow.
DECISION POINT
Reopen tomorrow? Staff trauma support? Media statement? Evidence preservation?

Scenario 3 — Foodborne Illness Outbreak
Multiple guests report illness · Moderate · 60 min
▼
Objectives: Public health coordination · Food safety protocols · Investigation procedures
Phase 1 — Initial Reports
INJECT
First aid reports 6 guests with GI symptoms — all ate at same F&B location 12–2 PM. By 9 PM, county health calls: 12 poison control calls from park visitors.
DECISION POINT
What food do you preserve? Close F&B tomorrow? Employee interviews tonight? Media statement?
Phase 2 — Investigation
INJECT
Health dept arrives — wants temp logs, employee health records, food samples, cleaning logs. Local news: "Food Poisoning Outbreak." Social media blowing up.
DECISION POINT
Public statement? What if logs are incomplete? Other venues safe to operate?
Phase 3 — Legal & Regulatory
INJECT
47 confirmed cases. 8 hospitalized. 3 lawsuits. Preliminary: Salmonella in undercooked chicken. Employee worked while symptomatic.
DECISION POINT
Disciplinary action? Policy changes? When do you reopen?

Scenario 4 — Child Abduction Attempt
Stranger attempts to leave with unaccompanied minor · High · 60 min
▼
Objectives: Code Yellow procedures · Law enforcement coordination · Exit control · Security camera review
Phase 1 — Initial Report
INJECT
Security stops adult male at main exit with girl (~6 yrs). Girl says quietly "I don't know him." Man says "this is my daughter."
DECISION POINT
Detain the adult? Call police immediately? Where do you take them? Who interviews the child? Close the exit?
Phase 2 — Developing Situation
INJECT
Man now says he's her uncle. No ID. Girl says "mommy is still in the park." Police want cameras reviewed.
DECISION POINT
Camera access? PA announcement for mother? Close exits? Media blackout?
Phase 3 — Resolution
INJECT
Mother found in restroom. Does NOT know the man. Man has no connection to family. Criminal charges likely.
DECISION POINT
Your liability? Child protective services? Guest trespass/ban? Policy changes to prevent recurrence?
