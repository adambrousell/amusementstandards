How LensIQ Works

Interpretation Layer.
Not Another Sensor.

LensIQ doesn't compete with accesso or Terabee. It reads the same infrastructure and translates operational data into risk intelligence that feeds AS 360 composite scores.

01
Tap Existing Feeds
Connect to Terabee REST API, Nola OAuth, Safari AI webhooks, SenSource Vea, Ellis EAVS events, accesso data, Mobaro checklists, or any RTSP camera. No new hardware.
02
Contextualize
Raw throughput becomes risk signal. 1,200 riders/hr at 2pm on a Tuesday is normal. 1,200 riders/hr at 8pm on a Saturday with 3 staff is a pressure point.
03
Triangulate
Cross-reference LensIQ throughput with VoxIntel radio traffic and OpsScan operator data. Agreement = confidence. Divergence = finding.
04
Score
Feed AS 360 OLI (Operational Load Index) in real time. RIDE Score, SPLASH Score, and PLAY Score update continuously from live operational data.

Signal Triangulation

Three Sources.
One Defensible Score.

LensIQ provides the throughput verification layer. VoxIntel provides radio intelligence. OpsScan provides operator behavior. When all three agree, confidence is high. When they diverge — that gap is the finding.

VoxIntel
Radio Intelligence
Passive RF monitoring captures ride-down events, signal codes, safety system failures, and resource allocation decisions. 203 transmissions captured on opening night from 7 miles away. No cooperation required.
OpsScan
Operator Behavior
QR scan-in, checklist completion tracking, voice incident reports, and anomaly detection. Captures what operators do and when — with AI watching for patterns that don't add up.
LensIQ
Throughput Verification
Platform-agnostic ingestion from accesso, Terabee, Nola, Safari AI, SenSource, Ellis EAVS, or raw RTSP feeds. Translates operational metrics into underwriting-grade risk intelligence. The interpretation layer the industry is missing.
When Signals Diverge — That's the Finding.
The power of triangulation isn't confirmation — it's contradiction detection. A divergence between sources is more valuable than agreement.
