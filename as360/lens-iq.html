Camera Integration

Works With What
You Already Have.

Three integration models cover every camera type in the market — from enterprise CCTV to consumer cloud cameras to on-demand spot checks.

Recommended
Cloud Camera API
For parks using Verkada, Avigilon, Hikvision HikConnect, or Reolink. Worker polls the camera API on a fixed cadence, pulls snapshots, sends to Claude Vision. Near-zero cost beyond existing camera subscription.
Requires: Cloud camera account credentials or API key
High Accuracy
RTSP Stream
Direct access to the camera's live RTSP stream on the local network. Edge device runs lightweight CV model in real time. Most accurate — continuous analysis rather than interval snapshots.
Requires: Network access + camera IP address
Lowest Friction
On-Demand Snapshot
Supervisor triggers a throughput check from the MGR module. System pulls one snapshot, Claude Vision returns a guest count. Not continuous — but more defensible than operator self-reporting.
Requires: Any accessible camera URL

AI Processing Layer

Claude Vision
at the Load Platform.

Claude Vision handles the controlled environment of a load platform with high reliability. Fixed camera angle, defined boarding zone, consistent lighting — purpose-built for accurate guest counting.

  • 01
    Guest Count per Cycle
    Counts guests in ride vehicles at the moment of dispatch. Returns headcount, seat utilization, and any anomalous observations as structured JSON.
  • 02
    Queue Depth Estimation
    Estimates queue length at defined intervals. Queue growth rate relative to throughput is a leading indicator of operational stress before it becomes visible to supervisors.
  • 03
    Operator Presence Check
    Flags frames where no operator is visible at the control panel position during dispatch — a passive safety audit with no checklist required.
  • 04
    Throughput Anomaly
    A ride averaging 8 cycles/hour dropping to 3 without an open CAR flags automatically — silent operational slowdown detected before any supervisor notices.

Signal Triangulation

Three Sources.
One Defensible Score.

LensIQ provides the third independent signal in the AS 360 data architecture. When all three layers agree, confidence is high. When they diverge — that gap is the finding.

VoxIntel
Radio Intelligence
Passive monitoring of radio traffic captures ride-down events, out-of-service calls, and operational status independent of any operator action. No cooperation required.
OpsScan
Operator Behavior
QR scan-in, voice incident reports, and anomaly detection capture what operators do and how they behave — with AI watching for patterns that don't add up.
LensIQ
Visual Throughput
Existing cameras independently verify cycle counts, headcounts, and operational tempo. No operator involvement. The camera sees what actually happens at the load platform.
When Signals Diverge — That's the Finding.
The power of triangulation isn't confirmation — it's contradiction detection. A divergence between sources is more valuable than agreement between them.
✓ All Three Agree
VoxIntel: ride operational · OpsScan: no incidents · LensIQ: normal TPH → High confidence · Score holds
⚠ Divergence Detected
VoxIntel: ride down 10:45 · OpsScan: no CAR opened · LensIQ: zero cycles 10:45–13:20 → Integrity flag · Unreported downtime
