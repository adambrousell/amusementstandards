AuditScore · Gamified Inspection Engine

Compliance Behavior. Measured. Ranked. Scored.

Converts inspection schedules into behavioral data. Operators earn completion metrics. Supervisors see teams. Ops Managers build streaks. Insurers see aggregate + anomaly flags. Every audit feeds the Readiness pillar.

OPERATOR
Own metrics, personal streak, shift rank. Can submit amendment requests.
SHIFT SUPERVISOR
Team completion rates, skip patterns, amendment approval queue.
OPS MANAGER
All staff, quality scores, anomaly flags, Manager Streak + badges.
INSPECTION COORD.
Audit completion per attraction vs. scheduled frequency.
INSURER
AuditScore index, 90-day completion, anomaly flags. No operator names.
How an Inspection Flows Through AuditScore
01
Checklist Assigned
Pre-open inspection auto-assigned to operator on shift. Attraction type determines checklist (coaster, flat, aquatic, play, water ride).
02
Operator Completes on Mobile
1–5 scoring per item. GPS + device fingerprint captured. Timer running — duration recorded for anomaly detection.
03
XP Earned · Streak Updated
+50 XP for on-time completion. +25 bonus for thoroughness (items scored 1–4 instead of all 5s). Streak counter increments.
04
Anomaly Engine Scans
Speed check (too fast?), variance check (always 5/5?), clustering check (batch submission?). Flags route to supervisor.
05
Pillar Score Updated
Completion rate, quality, anomaly flags all feed Readiness pillar in real time. Insurer sees aggregate — no names.

Audit Amendment Request

Corrections Without Compromising the Record

Originals never silently overwritten. Amendments create linked corrections. Insurers see both. Trail is immutable. Amendment frequency is an Integrity pillar input.

Open Amendment Form →
6-Step Chain of Custody
01
Operator Identifies Error
Opens amendment form. Three fields: which item, what was entered, what it should be.
02
Geo-Fence + Device Verified
GPS confirms operator at ride. Device fingerprint matches original audit phone.
03
Photo Evidence Required
Timestamped photo of the item — gauge reading, restraint position. EXIF preserved.
04
Original Record Sealed
Permanently locked. Cannot be edited by anyone. Amendment is a new linked record.
05
Supervisor Approval · 4hr Window
Routes to shift supervisor. 4 hours to approve/deny. One escalation attempt allowed.
06
Pattern Detection
5+ amendments in 30 days auto-flags training gap or gaming pattern.

Pilot Proposal

Three Phases. Zero Friction at Entry.

Phase 1 requires zero park involvement. Phase 2 introduces the platform as a free loss control benefit. Phase 3 adds signal depth as parks engage.

01
Insurer-Side Only
Week 1–2 · Zero park involvement
MASI provides:
3–5 insured facility roster. Loss runs under NDA. Loss control reports if available.
AS 360 delivers:
Desktop Assessment scoring from public data — state inspection records, incident history, news, operator questionnaire. Facility Score Certificate returned within 48 hours.
Park does:
Nothing. They don't know yet.
02
Park Opt-In
Week 2–6 · Premium incentive
MASI introduces:
Platform arrives as "MASI Loss Control Platform, powered by AS 360." Extension of existing insurer relationship.
Park gets (free):
Manager Module — pre-loaded with their Kronos shifts, Mobaro inspections. Facility Score Certificate with peer comparison.
03
Signal Escalation
Month 2–3 · Voluntary upgrade
Parks seeing value:
Upgrade to Tier 2 (camera-based tracking on existing CCTV) voluntarily. AuditScore competition between shifts.
AS 360 delivers:
Full triangulation + divergence detection. Quarterly compliance reviews. Portfolio risk comparison. Insurer Portal live.
The Adoption Model
Same approach as Progressive Snapshot (auto), Coalition (cyber), CompScience (workers' comp). The insurer offers a premium incentive tied to participation. Strong operators opt in because they benefit. Operators who decline — that's a signal too.
