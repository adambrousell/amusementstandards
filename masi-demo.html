Audit Amendment Request

Corrections Without Compromising the Record

Originals never silently overwritten. Amendments create linked corrections. Insurers see both. Trail is immutable. Amendment frequency is an Integrity pillar input.

Open Amendment Form →
6-Step Chain of Custody
01
Operator Identifies Error
Opens amendment form. Three fields: which item, what was entered, what it should be.
02
Geo-Fence + Device Verified
GPS confirms operator at ride. Device fingerprint matches original audit phone.
03
Photo Evidence Required
Timestamped photo of the item — gauge reading, restraint position. EXIF preserved.
04
Original Record Sealed
Permanently locked. Cannot be edited by anyone. Amendment is a new linked record.
05
Supervisor Approval · 4hr Window
Routes to shift supervisor. 4 hours to approve/deny. One escalation attempt allowed.
06
Pattern Detection
5+ amendments in 30 days auto-flags training gap or gaming pattern.
