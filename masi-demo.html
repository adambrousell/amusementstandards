What This Means
For Your Book.

AS 360 maps directly to the loss control gaps that exist across the current attractions insurance market. Each category below represents a risk area where existing underwriting relies on self-reported data, periodic audits, or no signal at all — and where AS 360 provides a continuous, independently-derived measurement.

Equipment Breakdown
Mechanical Integrity
Current signal: Loss history only. No pre-loss mechanical compliance data in the underwriting file at any stage of the policy lifecycle.
AS 360 Integrity pillar scores maintenance log compliance, NDT status, service bulletin adherence, and CAR closure rates — continuously, not at renewal.
GL · Water Parks
Aquatic Risk Gap
Current signal: No aquatics-specific loss control content in the specialty market. Generic GL applied to high-severity water park claims.
SPLASH Score is the only standalone aquatic risk index. 12 streams, 6 pillars — including a dedicated Lifeguard pillar, the leading variable in aquatic fatalities.
Operator Compliance
Behavioral Drift
Current signal: Periodic audit snapshots. No compliance data between audit dates. Compliance theater is indistinguishable from genuine compliance.
OpsScan captures behavioral timestamps at every scan-in. AI anomaly detection flags statistical patterns — speed, zero-variance, clustering — that indicate documentation gaming.
GL · FECs
FEC / PLAY Score Gap
Current signal: FECs treated with the same framework as traditional parks despite a different risk profile — youth patron exposure, inflatables, trampolines, laser tag, high throughput.
PLAY Score is the only FEC-specific risk index. Includes a standalone Youth pillar — the only quantified minor patron risk data available to any carrier in this market.
Operational Tempo
Throughput Pressure
Current signal: No operational loading data in any underwriting file. A park running at 120% capacity on a 95°F holiday weekend looks identical to a normal day.
LensIQ ingests CV-derived throughput from existing park cameras — independently. Capacity utilization and OLI feed the Delivery pillar in real time, no operator input.
Incident Response
Radio Intelligence
Current signal: Incident reports submitted post-event with no independent verification of response timing, communication quality, or unreported events.
VoxIntel passively monitors all venue radio channels. Ride-down events, response timing, step-on collision detection — an auditable operational timeline no operator can alter.
