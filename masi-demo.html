What This Means
For Your Book.

AS 360 maps directly to the loss control gaps that exist across the current attractions insurance market. Each of the following represents a risk category where existing underwriting relies on self-reported data, periodic audits, or no data at all — and where AS 360 provides a continuous, independently-derived signal.

Equipment Breakdown
Mechanical Integrity
Current signal: Loss history only. No pre-loss mechanical compliance data in the underwriting file.
AS 360 Integrity pillar scores maintenance log compliance, NDT status, service bulletin adherence, and CAR closure rates — continuously, not at renewal.
General Liability · Water Parks
Aquatic Risk Gap
Current signal: No aquatics-specific loss control content exists in the MASI resources library. Generic GL framework applied to high-severity water park claims.
SPLASH Score is the only standalone aquatic risk index in the industry. 12 streams, 6 pillars including a dedicated Lifeguard pillar — the leading cause of aquatic fatalities, now scored continuously.
Operator Compliance
Behavioral Drift
Current signal: Periodic audit snapshots. No data on operator compliance between audit dates. Compliance theater is indistinguishable from genuine compliance.
OpsScan captures behavioral timestamps from every scan-in, sign-off, and incident report. AI anomaly detection flags statistical patterns that indicate documentation gaming — not self-reported checkboxes.
GL · FECs & Entertainment
FEC / PLAY Score Gap
Current signal: FECs treated the same as traditional amusement parks despite different risk profile — youth patron exposure, go-karts, inflatables, high-volume throughput.
PLAY Score is the only FEC-specific risk index. Includes a standalone Youth pillar — the only quantified minor patron risk data in the market. 10 streams, 4 pillars.
Operational Tempo
Throughput Pressure
Current signal: No operational loading data in any underwriting file. A park running rides at 120% capacity on a 95°F holiday weekend looks identical to normal operations.
LensIQ ingests CV-derived throughput from existing park cameras — independently, without operator involvement. Capacity utilization and OLI feed the RIDE Score Delivery pillar continuously.
Incident Response
Radio Intelligence
Current signal: Incident reports submitted post-event by operators. No independent verification of response timing, communication quality, or unreported events.
VoxIntel passively monitors all venue radio channels. Ride-down events, response times, and step-on collision detection are captured independently — creating an auditable operational timeline no operator can alter.

The Scoring Output

Three Indices.
Every Attraction Type.

Each AS 360 score index is designed for a specific attraction type. A coaster gets a RIDE Score. A wave pool gets a SPLASH Score. A play structure gets a PLAY Score. A hybrid attraction scores across multiple indices. Every score is composited from independent data streams — not self-reported surveys.

Data Acquisition Layer

Three Independent Signals.
One Defensible Score.

The critical property of AS 360 for underwriting purposes is that its three primary data acquisition layers cannot be gamed simultaneously. OpsScan captures operator behavior. LensIQ captures what cameras independently see. VoxIntel captures what radio traffic independently says. When all three agree, the score is high-confidence. When they diverge — that gap is the finding.
