Top driver: Setup/teardown failure + undertrained seasonal staff.
Resorts with Attractions

Hotel waterparks, adventure courses, zip lines. All three scores active = AS 360 Composite.
Top driver: Unattended attraction injury + resort pool staffing gaps.
