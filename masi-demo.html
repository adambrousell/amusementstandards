CAR Portal. Multi-Layer. Insurer-Grade.

Five-stage review workflow from field submission to verified closure. Every corrective action is timestamped, assigned, tracked, and scored into the RIDE / SPLASH / PLAY composite. Gamified to reward the operators who surface problems — not hide them.

Nothing closes without verification.
