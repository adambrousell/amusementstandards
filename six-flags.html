AS 360 Platform Overview
Q1 2026
AS 360 is a park operations platform that produces a scored risk index.
Not a survey. Not an audit checklist. AS 360 is a complete operational management system — three real-time field modules your teams use every shift, 19 inspection and audit forms, structured incident reporting, QR-based guest feedback, and automated data feeds. The scored risk index is the output of running the park on the system, not a separate exercise.

How the Modules Connect
The three field modules aren't independent apps — they share data in real time. When an operator completes an opening checklist in the Operator Log, the Manager's clear-to-open board updates. When a rotation timer hits critical in Manager, the Dispatcher gets the alert. When emergency response roles are assigned in Manager at shift start, they push live to the Dispatcher's board. Corrective actions opened from any inspection form flow into the Manager's CAR pipeline with aging buckets and escalation triggers.

The 19 inspection forms live inside the Manager Module's Inspections tab — not as separate tools, but as part of the same interface the manager is already using to run their shift. That's the design principle: data collection is embedded in daily operations, not layered on top of them.

When all three scores are active at one facility, they produce a Park Intelligence Composite (PIC) — a single cross-validated index covering every guest-facing zone. Designed for portfolio operators and underwriting placement.
What This Means at Portfolio Scale
Same modules, same forms, same scoring methodology across every facility. A RIDE Score at one park is directly comparable to a RIDE Score at another — same streams, same pillar math. That's the foundation for cross-facility benchmarking, trend analysis, and a single portfolio-level risk view that doesn't depend on reconciling incompatible data from different sites.
