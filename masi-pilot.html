TRICK QUESTIONS TOM WILL ASK
"How is this different from what ODA already gives us?"
ODA gives pass/fail on a single day. AS 360 gives continuous composite across 15+ streams. Don't dismiss the regulator — Tom works alongside them. Say: "ODA validates compliance at a point in time. We measure operational behavior between those visits."
"Who validates your methodology?"
No one external yet. Own it. "The pilot IS the validation process. That's why we're here — to calibrate against what your team already knows about these facilities."
"What happens when your score and our assessment disagree?"
"The gap is the finding. When AS 360 flags something your team didn't see, or vice versa — that's where we learn the most. We're adding a data layer underneath your judgment, not replacing it."
"Can you handle our full book?"
"The pilot is 3–5 facilities. We deliver those with quality, calibrate with your feedback, then scale. Demo-ready for 10, infrastructure-ready for 500."
"What does implementation look like for the park?"
"Phase 1 — nothing. Zero park involvement. We score from public data and your loss control records. Phase 2 — MASI introduces it as a free loss control technology benefit of their policy. The park gets the Manager Module branded under MASI."
"What does this cost?"
Don't lead with price. "The pilot is at our cost — we're investing in the validation. Once we prove correlation, we price per-facility annually as a loss control analytics layer." If pressed: $200–$500/facility/year range for continuous scoring. CompScience charges comparable.
