The Credential

What a Certified Ride Controller Actually Is

One person per installation, per site. The manufacturer's designated qualified person of record — accountable before the first guest boards every operating day.

Who Gets Certified
The Site-Level Responsible Person
The CRC is the person who owns daily ride oversight — pre-opening inspection sign-off, restraint verification accountability, and the first decision-maker in any emergency. One per installation per site. Not every operator — the accountable person.
What the Course Covers
Ride-Specific + ASTM-Mapped
Full ride systems · Pre-opening inspection (§7.1) · Operator qualification (§8.1) · Daily duties (§8.2) · Field demonstration requirements (§8.3) · LOTO per OSHA 1910.147 · Emergency procedures · Incident command structure. Every module tied to the specific ride.
What It Does For RMC / Larson
A Documented Standard at Every Installation
Right now, every park running your equipment defines "qualified operator" on their own. The CRC creates one manufacturer-authored standard across your fleet. If an incident happens, there's a paper trail from the certified operator back to your OEM documentation — not the park's binder.
