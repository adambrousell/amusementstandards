The Problem You Didn't Know You Had

Every Park Running Your Ride Is Operating Under a Standard You Never Set

When a park takes delivery of your ride, they write their own training binder — or nothing at all. Every installation in your fleet is defining "qualified operator" independently. That's not their problem. That's yours.

One incident at a park running your equipment, with no documented manufacturer training standard on record, puts you in a very difficult conversation with your attorneys and insurers. The paper trail stops at delivery.

The Certified Ride Controller program changes that. Your equipment. Your certification standard. Your documented qualified person of record at every installation in the fleet.

The Gap
Every installation in your fleet is defining "qualified operator" independently. No manufacturer standard anywhere in the chain.
The Risk
In the absence of a manufacturer training program, incident liability shifts to design and engineering. A documented CRC program closes that gap before the incident happens.
The Opportunity
No other coaster or flat ride manufacturer offers ride-specific operator certification under their brand. First to build it owns the standard. Caterpillar did this over 30 years. No one in amusement has done it yet.

Parks access certification through a branded portal at a URL you control. Operators complete the course, pass the exam, and receive a certificate under your name. Every quarter, Amusement Standards delivers a fleet-wide certification report: every active CRC across every installation, pass/fail records, upcoming recertification dates, and lapsed certifications flagged. Same document is your billing artifact and your compliance record. Parks cannot underreport — the data comes from our system.

At annual renewal, you invoice the park at whatever rate you choose. Amusement Standards invoices you at our floor price. The difference is yours. Your relationship with the park stays intact. AS has no visibility or claim above our floor. Most parks will not know Amusement Standards is involved unless you choose to tell them.

The Certification Program

Three Levels. One Platform. Every Role Covered.

The CRC is the required credential and the anchor of the program. Attendant and Operator add-ons extend training to every person who touches the ride.

🎯 Certified Ride Controller
Required · AS-Hosted Portal
Who it's for
The site-level responsible person. Accountable for ensuring the ride is operated in conformance with manufacturer guidelines. One required per installation per site — the manufacturer's designated qualified person of record.
What it covers
Full ride systems · ASTM F770-18 §7.1/§8.1/§8.2/§8.3 · LOTO · Emergency procedures · Incident command · Field demo documentation. 80% pass threshold.
Pricing model

$500/ride/yr platform fee
🔵 Operator
Optional Add-On · SCORM Package
Who it's for
Staff who run the ride day-to-day — responsible for pre-opening inspection and operational decisions during the ride cycle. Not the site-level CRC.
What it covers
Pre-opening inspection per ASTM §7.1 · Ride systems overview · Rider screening · Load/unload sequence · Emergency stop and evacuation · Operational documentation.
Pricing model
$2,500 flat build fee per ride type
Unlimited learners
SCORM zip delivered to park
No per-learner tracking
🟢 Attendant
Optional Add-On · SCORM Package
Who it's for
Seasonal and part-time staff who load guests, check restraints, and monitor the queue. Guest-facing basics with no operational decision-making responsibility.
What it covers
Ride zone awareness · Restraint verification · Height and health restriction protocols · Guest communication · Queue management · When and how to stop the ride.
Pricing model
$1,500 flat build fee per ride type
Unlimited learners
SCORM zip delivered to park
No per-learner tracking
Why CRC is hosted and add-ons are SCORM

The CRC is the compliance record you need to verify — it's the billing basis and the liability documentation. AS hosting it means the data is always clean and always accessible to you. Attendant and Operator packages are park operations decisions. Delivering those as SCORM zip files respects the park's existing LMS investment and keeps AS infrastructure focused on the data that matters.
