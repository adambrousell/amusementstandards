Per-Site Audit Scope

What every standard compliance audit covers

Each site receives a structured on-site audit mapped directly to the service bulletin requirements. Scope is customized to the specific bulletin — the framework below applies to every engagement.

  • Operational Procedures Review Verify that the operator's manual has been updated per bulletin requirements. Confirm the original procedures have been replaced and are no longer accessible to staff.
  • Hardware / Equipment Inspection Physical inspection of any equipment modifications, component replacements, or configuration changes specified in the bulletin. Verified against manufacturer specifications.
  • Operator Competency Spot-Check Live observation of operator loading, dispatch, and/or maintenance procedures to confirm frontline staff understand and can execute the updated requirements.
  • Photo Documentation Before/after photographic evidence for each verification point. Time-stamped, geo-tagged where applicable, and included in the audit record.
  • Individual Site Compliance Report Pass/fail status for each audit checkpoint, narrative findings, and specific recommendations. Delivered within 5 business days of the on-site visit.
  • Portfolio Compliance Dashboard Aggregated, real-time compliance status across all audited sites. Color-coded pass/fail/pending view with drill-down capability for manufacturer leadership.
