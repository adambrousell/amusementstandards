Who This Is For

Risk Intelligence Pro is built for operators, insurers, and brokers who want to move beyond gut feel and anecdotes. If you're sitting on years of incident data but not fully using it, this program turns it into a decision‑making asset.

What’s Included

  • Full incident data analysis (12–24 months)
  • Trend mapping and pattern identification
  • Risk exposure heatmap by ride, location, or category
  • Documentation quality review
  • Strategic recommendations for prevention and training

Outcomes

  • Clear visibility into where and why incidents occur
  • Prioritized risk areas backed by data, not opinion
  • Better conversations with leadership, insurers, and regulators
  • A roadmap for targeted training and operational changes

Engagement Format

Duration: Flat‑rate, 3–4 week engagement

Delivery: Remote data review with optional on‑site briefing

Deliverables: Data analysis deck, heatmaps, and executive summary
