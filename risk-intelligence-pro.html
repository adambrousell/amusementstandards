Overview

Who This Program Is For

Risk Intelligence Pro is built for operators, insurers, and brokers who want to move beyond gut feel and anecdotes. If you're sitting on years of incident data but not fully using it, this program turns it into a decision‑making asset.

Program Scope

What’s Included

📊

Full Incident Data Analysis

We analyze 12–24 months of incident data for patterns and risk indicators.

  • Trend mapping
  • Root cause patterns
  • Seasonal and operational correlations
🔥

Risk Exposure Heatmap

Visualize where your highest‑risk areas truly are.

  • Ride‑level heatmaps
  • Location‑based risk scoring
  • Behavioral indicators
📝

Documentation Quality Review

We evaluate the completeness and consistency of your incident reporting.

  • Classification accuracy
  • Missing data detection
  • Recommendations for improvement

Deliverables

  • Data analysis deck
  • Risk heatmaps
  • Executive summary
  • Strategic recommendations
