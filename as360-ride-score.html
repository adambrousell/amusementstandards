The Four Pillars

Every letter answers a different question about your operation

Each pillar scores 0–100, fed by multiple independent data streams. Together they form a composite that captures the full operational and safety picture.

R
Readiness
Are you operationally prepared to run today?
The question every GM should ask at rope drop: "If something goes wrong in the next hour, are we set up to handle it?" Readiness scores staffing, dispatch efficiency, emergency protocols, and communication systems — adjusted for real-time operational pressure.
82
Operations are solid. Dispatch running clean, staff in position, emergency systems ready.
58
Operations are strained. High wait times, dispatch gaps observed, emergency preparedness has holes.
I
Integrity
Are your assets and infrastructure sound?
The physical plant score. Are rides mechanically sound? Are structures maintained? Are barriers intact? This is the score an insurer cares about most — ride availability, safety infrastructure condition, and structural soundness across the entire facility.
94
Physical plant excellent. Rides running, structures maintained, safety infrastructure intact.
61
Infrastructure concerns. Multiple rides down, walkway hazards noted, barrier condition issues.
D
Delivery
Is the guest experience being executed?
The experience quality score. Service interactions, food quality, cleanliness, queue experience — the things guests write reviews about. This is where exit survey data, online reviews, and guest service audits converge into a single performance measure.
87
Guest experience strong. Clean, friendly, good food service. Reviews align with audits.
63
Guest experience gaps. Food service slow, signage lacking, exit surveys and reviews confirm issues.
E
Environment
Is the physical environment safe and comfortable?
The conditions score. Weather-adjusted safety, restroom quality, ADA compliance, lighting — factors that affect guest comfort and safety independent of ride operations. This is where real-time weather data becomes actionable intelligence.
91
Environment excellent. Restrooms clean, conditions favorable, no weather concerns.
52
Environmental stress. Heat advisory conditions, restroom issues, slip hazards from rain.
Every score comes with a confidence level
Each pillar includes a convergence indicator that tells you how strongly the underlying data streams agree. High convergence means every source is telling the same story. Lower convergence means there's data disagreement worth investigating — the score is directionally correct, but the picture is more nuanced.
This is what separates RIDE from a single-source audit score. When multiple independent streams converge on the same conclusion, you can act with confidence.

How It Works

From your data to your dashboard

The RIDE Score integrates with your existing audit and reporting infrastructure — we add the analytical layer, not another tool to manage.

01
Connect Your Data Streams
We ingest your existing audit data, safety reports, exit survey results, incident logs, and near-miss reports. Weather and queue time data feeds automatically. No new data collection required — we work with what you already have.
02
AS 360 Computes the Scores
Our convergence engine processes every stream, maps inputs to the four RIDE pillars, computes convergence confidence, and generates per-period scores with trend analysis. Built in R. Reproducible. Auditable.
03
Scores Delivered via API
Four pillar scores, convergence confidence, trend direction, contributing sources, and weather snapshot — transmitted to your dashboard every audit period. Compatible with overVUE and custom integrations. Your GM reads four numbers, not a 40-page report.
