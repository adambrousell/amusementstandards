THE PROCESS
Three phases.
One score.
Amusement Standards deploys AS 360 across your facility. Every data stream is independent — no single source controls the outcome. When sources agree, confidence is high. When they diverge, that gap is the finding.
01
🏗️
Deploy
Amusement Standards embeds AS 360 across your operation. Operator documentation streams are activated, audit schedules are set, and automated data feeds are connected. No disruption to daily operations.
02
📡
Collect
Fifteen independent streams feed the system simultaneously — from your team on the floor, your management layer, independent AS auditors deployed without staff knowledge, your guests in real time, and automated live feeds. No single source controls the outcome.
03
📊
Score
All streams converge into four pillar scores — Readiness, Integrity, Delivery, Environment — which aggregate into a single composite RIDE Score. Insurers receive a structured risk dossier. Operators receive a prioritized action list.
THE FRAMEWORK
Four pillars.
360° coverage.
Every stream feeds one or more pillars. Pillar scores are independent — a strong Readiness score does not mask a weak Integrity score. The composite reflects all four dimensions simultaneously.
